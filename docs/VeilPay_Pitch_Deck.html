01 · The ProblemWhy Transparent Chains Fail Payroll

Transparent Blockchains Break Enterprise Payroll

Transparent Chains (Ethereum / Solana / L2s)

  • Paying contributors publicly broadcasts every employee's exact salary to coworkers, competitors, recruiters, and phishing attackers.
  • Permanently links personal employee wallets to employer treasury schedules and burn rate.

Opaque Privacy Mixers

  • Fails DAO governance: token holders cannot verify payroll stayed inside approved budget caps or compensation bands.
  • Fails audits & real life: employees cannot prove income for mortgages or visas; auditors cannot verify aggregate spend.
Result: 90%+ of crypto-native DAOs and foundations still fall back to opaque centralized Web2 payroll rails.

02 · Why MidnightDual-Ledger Rational Privacy

Built Natively for Midnight's Dual-Ledger ZK Model

VeilPay is not an EVM port—it relies directly on Compact 0.28 primitives unique to Midnight:

Public Ledger (ledger)

Enforces global governance rules (budgetCap, salary bands, headcount, lifecycle status) while storing only opaque commitments and unlinkable nullifiers.

Local Private State (witness)

Keeps secret keys, individual salaries, employee identities, and 32-byte blinding salts strictly on each participant's device.

Compile-Time Disclosure Safety (disclose())

Compact 0.28 statically prevents accidental leakage of private witness values into public ledger state transitions.

Programmable Selective Disclosure

Participants reveal only the exact mathematical proof a counterparty needs (threshold income or aggregate spend)—nothing more.

03 · Product WalkthroughEnd-to-End 5-Step Lifecycle

How VeilPay Works End-to-End

Step 1
createPayrollRun
Treasury sets public governance bounds ($35k cap, $4k–$18k band, headcount 3) + hidden $0 accumulator commitment.
Step 2
issueVoucher
ZK circuit proves min ≤ salary ≤ max and total + salary ≤ cap without publishing any salary or running sum. Appends commitment to HistoricMerkleTree<10>.
Step 3
claimVoucher
Employee proves Merkle membership privately and posts an unlinkable nullifier—breaking issuance-to-claim linkage and blocking double-claims.
Step 4
proveIncomeThreshold
Employee proves salary ≥ $10,000 for a bank or landlord while exact $12,750 pay stays 100% hidden.
Step 5
discloseRunTotal
Post-finalizeRun, authorized Compliance Auditor reveals only aggregate spend ($29,000)—individual salaries stay shielded.

04 · Cryptographic ArchitectureCompact 0.28 Security Design

Cryptographic Architecture & Security Guarantees

8 Real Compiled ZK Circuits

Full zkir-v3 proving and verifying keys compiled in 15.8s via native compactc 0.36.1 (8 impure + 7 pure circuits, zero errors, zero warnings).

Zero ownPublicKey() Dependence

All role authorization derives keys via persistentHash([domain, orgId, sk]); employee keys, nullifiers, and attestations bind to kernel.self().bytes to eliminate cross-contract replay.

Dictionary-Attack-Resistant Commitments

Uses persistentCommit with mandatory fresh 32-byte salts (newSalt != prevSalt) so low-entropy integer salaries can never be brute-forced from public state.

Concurrent-Safe HistoricMerkleTree<10>

Supports up to 1,024 vouchers per vault while keeping in-flight employee Merkle proofs valid across concurrent issuances.
